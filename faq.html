Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common notary questions

What is a notary public?
A notary public is a state-commissioned official who verifies the identity of document signers, witnesses signatures, and helps prevent fraud. Notaries administer oaths and certify copies of certain documents.
What do I need to bring to my appointment?
Bring a valid, unexpired photo ID (driver's license, passport, or state ID), the document(s) to be notarized (unsigned — do not sign beforehand), any required witnesses, and payment.
Do you come to my location?
Yes! We are a mobile notary service. We travel to your home, office, hospital, care facility, or any convenient location within our service area. A travel fee applies.
How much does it cost?
General notarizations are $15 per signature. Loan signings start at $125. Mobile travel fees start at $35 within 25 miles. See our Pricing page for full details.
Can you notarize documents after hours or on weekends?
Yes, we offer after-hours and weekend appointments for an additional $50 fee. Contact us to check availability.
What types of documents can you notarize?
We notarize a wide range of documents including real estate documents, loan packages, powers of attorney, affidavits, contracts, adoption papers, and more.
Can I upload my documents online?
Yes! Use our secure document upload page to send your documents before your appointment. Your files are encrypted and password-protected.
What is an apostille?
An apostille is a certification that authenticates a document for use in another country. We can help prepare your documents and guide you through the apostille process.
How do I schedule an appointment?
You can schedule an appointment by visiting our Contact page, calling us, or sending an email. We respond within 2 hours during business hours.
