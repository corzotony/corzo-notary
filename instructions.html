How It Works

Simple, straightforward, and convenient

1

Contact Us

Reach out via our contact form, phone, or email. Tell us what you need notarized and your preferred location and time.

2

Get a Quote

We'll provide a transparent quote including notary fees and travel costs. No hidden charges, no surprises.

3

Upload Documents (Optional)

Use our secure upload page to send your documents ahead of time so we can review them before we arrive.

4

We Come to You

Our notary arrives at your chosen location at the scheduled time. We verify your ID, witness your signature, and complete the notarization.

5

Done!

You receive your notarized documents on the spot. We can also provide a digital copy or mail additional copies if needed.
